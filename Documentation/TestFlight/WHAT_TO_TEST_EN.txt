# Mikansei 0.7.2 Beta Test Notes

## TestFlight update text

This hotfix restores registration for four iOS Home Screen widgets: Today Checklist, Quick Add, Today Progress, and Focus Timer. Confirm that all four appear in the widget gallery and can be added. Also check that deadline and focus Live Activities still work.

## What to test

- If you already have a legacy widget on the Home Screen or Lock Screen, update in place, open Mikansei once, then check that the widget and its content appear in the original location.
- Search for Mikansei in the widget gallery and check that all four restored widgets and the Pomodoro widget can be added. Try quick add or timer controls.
- In Settings, enable automatic display of today’s deadlines. Confirm the deadline Live Activity appears on the Lock Screen and opens the relevant item. When a focus Live Activity is also active, confirm the two activities remain independent.
- Start a focus session with a subject. Check the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island on supported devices; try pause, resume, and finish and save.
- Include the device model, iOS version, steps, and results in feedback. Redact personal information in screenshots.
