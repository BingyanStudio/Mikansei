Mikansei is a native iOS app for tasks, schedules, and notes. Create tasks, plan schedules, add checklist steps, and take notes. Optional integrations include Apple Calendar, widgets, Live Activities, and reminders.

You can use the app offline without signing in; your data stays on your device. Optional email-code sign-in is shared with Mikanseta. Requesting a code for a new email can create an account; sign-in requires verification. Before the first sign-in, the app asks you to confirm merging local content with the account’s cloud archive. When the cloud service is enabled, signed-in content can sync between iOS and Mac. iCloud Drive folder sync is a separate option. Offline use remains available when cloud service is unavailable.

Please report issues through TestFlight feedback or email kikuchiheinr@qq.com with your device model, iOS version, and reproduction steps. Remove personal information from screenshots or descriptions before sending.
